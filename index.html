ShiftLingo support

Workplace phrase rehearsal for iPhone. The app is being prepared for App Store release; this page is not an App Store availability announcement.

Get help

Email support@technicaldost.com. Include the app version, your iOS version and a description of the problem. You do not need to send personal phrase notes.

Restore Pro

Use Settings → Restore purchases with the Apple Account that made the purchase. If no active subscription is found, check your Apple subscriptions and purchase history.

Manage or cancel

Manage subscriptions with Apple. Deleting the app or its local data does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Offline and audio

Phrase practice is offline. Store purchases, restoration, external links and voice downloads need connectivity. Listening uses system voices available on your iPhone. There is no microphone recording or automated pronunciation grade.

Export or delete data

Open Settings → Your data. Export JSON before deleting if you want a copy. The export includes personal notes. App deletion can remove local progress; there is no developer-operated cloud sync. Device backups follow your Apple settings.

What is included?

8 free first-day phrases. Optional Pro unlocks 10 packs, 80 original model phrases, personal cards and review across the complete library. Standard German with Swiss spelling conventions, not a Swiss dialect course. No account, ads or tracking SDK.
