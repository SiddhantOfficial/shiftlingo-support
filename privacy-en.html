Privacy policy

ShiftLingo does not require an account and includes no advertising, tracking SDK or developer analytics service. Practice progress, favorites, settings and personal phrases are stored locally in the app container. No microphone recording is requested. Listening uses system speech voices; voice availability depends on your device.

Purchases and subscription verification are processed by Apple using StoreKit. Apple may collect transaction, account and diagnostic information under its own policies. We do not receive your payment card details. External policy or support links open websites with their own privacy practices.

Settings lets you export your local data or permanently delete it. An exported file contains any personal notes you wrote; choose its destination carefully. Deleting the app or its data does not cancel a subscription. Manage subscriptions through Apple.

Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. There is no developer-operated cloud sync. This policy reflects version 1.0 and is dated 3 November 2026.
